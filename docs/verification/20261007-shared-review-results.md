## Decision

**Do not adopt the candidate as a verified retention repair or mining improvement.**
Its sustained shared-learner review contrast did occur, and both arms passed all
predeclared frozen retention floors. But the required matched-control loss did
not reproduce: ordinary wooden-pickaxe scores were parent **29/32 and 30/32**,
control **27/32 and 27/32**, candidate **26/32 and 27/32**. Control losses of two
and three were not the required loss of at least eight on each seed. The candidate
had no nominal crafting advantage. All mining completion scores remained zero.

The conjunctive hypothesis therefore remains unsupported. This is **not** a
candidate retention-floor failure, an incomplete experiment or evidence that
review is universally ineffective. It distinguishes demonstrated delivery of
rehearsal from a demonstrated behavioral benefit. No extra segment, sample-budget
extension, replacement seed, altered floor or deployment followed this result.
The parent and both complete trained states are preserved, not overwritten.

The post-initial observed interval contained **19.547747%** earlier-task accepted
samples in candidate and **0%** in control. Every candidate earlier task had at
least 831 accepted samples in that late interval, above the declared 100 floor.
Thus this time the missing exposure contrast is not the reason the causal
retention explanation fails. A shared-data mechanism can work operationally
without establishing a learned advantage.

## A new endpoint, not a reversal of the previous failed qualification

The [previous individual-coverage qualification](20261007-review-admission-results.md)
remains failed: candidate 7/8, control 6/8. Its actor 136 missing tasks 1, 2 and 7,
original budget, thresholds, archive and closed-unmerged PR #60 are unchanged.
That offline qualification started no new learning. Its individual full-cycle
criterion is not silently dropped from its original decision or declared passed.

Reading the current implementation established one `Learner` and one published
training `Policy` shared across ordinary actors. Their bodies and earned course
records are separate, while exam policies are frozen snapshots. This justified a
separately declared, narrower live question: whether **accepted contexts for that
shared learner** plus a reproduced control loss support frozen-policy retention.
It did not justify claiming every actor completes every earlier task within a
finite window. Such a guarantee may matter for individual learners, contexts or
future autonomous lives and remains unresolved here.

The new endpoint and its justification were recorded before these fresh trials.
No runtime knob was retuned: candidate core/plugin/training sources and JAR bytes
are identical to the previously measured admission implementation. We used its
known short opportunity feasibility to test a different hypothesis, not new
replay seeds or a longer replay to make the old screen pass. The live exposure
and frozen retention margins were the same as the earlier cohort-credit study.

## Mining diagnosis: having a pickaxe is not effective tool use

Each mining report had 19,200 decision observations. All reports had zero broken
blocks and zero ordinary mining successes. The first parent seed had one fleeting
target-pick contact observation and four maximum target-pick ticks; the second
parent and all four stopped-policy reports had zero contact and zero maximum
pick ticks. Do not erase that small parent observation or call it acquisition.

Candidate had more held-pick observations, but its personal inventory was open
in roughly 96% of observations and effective world-dig choices were scarce.

These are frozen correlates, not evidence that rehearsal caused the GUI pattern
or that automatically closing the menu would solve the task. No scripted rescue,
auto-selection, imitation or teacher behavior was added.
