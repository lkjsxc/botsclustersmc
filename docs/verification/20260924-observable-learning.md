## The aiming correction and its limits

Near-target reset assistance alone did not resolve the observed aiming plateau.
Dense angular-error costs alone also failed within their observed window.
Introducing short-settling practice before the full 20-tick condition produced
full-condition successes. This sequential intervention is not a randomized
ablation isolating the causal contribution of each change.

[Aiming practice](../AIMING.md) documents the exact reset, duration and reward
rules. Probe/exam initial angles, random draws, 20-tick duration and passing
thresholds were not relaxed. No pathfinding, auto-aim, teacher action, crafting
macro, imitation data or LLM selects normal gameplay actions.

## Survival and cooperation are not completed

The 512-value local input interface avoids per-agent chunk transfer and supplies
bounded local terrain, motion, inventory and nearby-entity state. That interface
is a foundation, not a learned social policy or a global planning system.

The bodies are not logged-in players. Hunger, complete combat/tool durability,
all vanilla recipes, restart-persistent NPC lives, autonomous long-horizon goal
selection and a functioning cooperative settlement remain incomplete or
unverified. Neither the 18 scripted fixtures nor these four motor tasks prove
ordinary shared survival. Training can continue through the independent course,
but no later-task mastery or eventual completion is guaranteed by elapsed time.
