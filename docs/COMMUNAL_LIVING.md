# Communal living, not a crowd of independent task executors

The product target is many learned citizens maintaining one shared settlement.
Population, movement, historical task certificates and scripted demonstrations
are not substitutes for sustained provision, useful sharing and recovery.
The current deployment is still a task-conditioned NPC experiment. It does not
implement this target, an autonomous goal selector or persistent citizen lives.

## Measured source-only supply baseline

The [closed supply comparison](verification/20261008-communal-supply-results.md)
now completed three independent visible/hidden-demand learning pairs and 24,576
frozen synthetic trials. Actual Pocket shift-clicks, a one-slot common store and
irreversible requested consumption were used; no Minecraft server or deployed
model participated. Visible-demand two-step completion was 45.31%-49.22%, only
0-1.5625 percentage points above paired hidden controls. Eight-member transfer
was 23.44%-30.08%. All declared gates failed. Public demand and common reward are
therefore a measured insufficient mechanism under this exact implementation and
budget, not an established communal learner. Retain this baseline when testing
credit assignment or better demand conditioning; do not just increase population.

## Needs before occupations

The preferred next design is a public needs field: a settlement exposes what it
needs and what usable common stock is actually known to be present. It does not
assign a citizen the role of miner, supply a recipe sequence, expose another
citizen's private pocket or issue an equipment/action macro. The learned policy
must choose whether to acquire, produce, carry, share, consume or defer work.
Publicly designated stores are infrastructure, not a global omniscient inventory.
Missing or stale observations remain unknown, not zero stock or free reward.

This is deliberately different from coordinating a crowd with a central scripted
job dispatcher. Start with a small inspectable community; do not convert the
existing 512 isolated training rooms into a claim of 512 collaborating citizens.
The unmerged shared-needs and commons-exam research branches remain separate:
a numeric sidecar, accounting tests or an untrained neural trial do not qualify
a deployed community learner. No research schema is silently relabelled as the
current 512-float policy input.

## The settlement is the unit of success

Measure sustained coverage of real demand, its worst material-specific shortage,
useful consumption, replenishment, resource loss and recovery after disruption.
Retain the identities of consumed and replaced resources in the measurement.
A chest deposit is not automatically a social contribution: the same stack must
not earn repeated production credit by circling between pockets and a chest.
A high average supply must not hide a material that nobody ever provides.
A common return and an individual's causal contribution are different quantities.
Neither dividing a return by population nor counting more actors establishes
optimizer-scale invariance or cooperative competence.

Use sustained demand changes and actual useful withdrawals, not just a reward
for keeping every tool locked in storage. The environment can declare needs and
initial infrastructure, but must disclose supplied resources, protected stations,
invulnerability and other mechanical assistance. It must not secretly execute
missing steps of the citizens' plan.

## Deliberately adversarial social qualification

A useful unconventional test is to shuffle the society rather than merely change
the world seed. Replace a partner, swap initial resources and mirror the station
layout. Later vary population and remove a productive member. A learned society
should restore provision without relying on a privileged citizen ID, a permanent
scripted pair or knowledge of another actor's private state.

Predeclare these comparisons before learning, alongside isolated and pooled-stock
controls, independent learning seeds, fixed-policy trials and retained individual
skills. Keep failures and all conditions. Shared resources should improve outcomes
when interdependence is real; extra physical bodies alone are not the treatment.
This proposal is not an implemented role-switcher or a passed experiment.

## Mechanical integrity is a deployment prerequisite

Ordinary public storage must preserve supported items through competing primitive
clicks. Native locks and deferred loot are not public stock. The
[shared-resource boundary](SHARED_RESOURCES.md) rejects protected access and
simplified mining instead of unlocking storage, generating loot or discarding its
block-entity contents. This does not claim complete vanilla permission semantics,
arbitrary protection-plugin integration or durable pocket/world transactions.

Do not expand the number of interacting actors until finite tests show both
resource conservation and useful learned coordination. Keep the production
inference artifact free of exam controllers, curriculum resets and optimizer
code. Durable citizen identity, pockets and a consistent world/save boundary are
still separate prerequisites for a settlement that persists across restarts.
